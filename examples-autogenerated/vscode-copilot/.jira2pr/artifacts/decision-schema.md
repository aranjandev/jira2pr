# Decision Schema

A decision document captures an important technical choice that should remain visible throughout the workflow.

## Question

Describe the decision being evaluated.

## Recommendation

State the selected option.

## Alternatives Considered

For each option:

- Name
- Pros
- Cons

## Rationale

Explain why the recommendation was chosen.

## Constraints

List assumptions, requirements, cost considerations, operational concerns, or environmental constraints that influenced the decision.

## Risks

Identify notable risks or tradeoffs associated with the recommendation.

## References

Relevant documentation, articles, tickets, or source material.
